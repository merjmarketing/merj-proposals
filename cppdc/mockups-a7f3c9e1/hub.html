Employer Connect Hub

Curated information, a wider network and a stronger talent pipeline for Texas employers who recruit, develop and retain skilled talent through adult education partnerships.

Be a Change Agent
Explore the Hub
What it is. The Employer Connect Hub is CPPDC's online home for Texas employers. It collects curated information on hiring and training through adult education, connects employers with the adult education and literacy providers, workforce boards and partners in their region, and strengthens the talent pipeline from classroom to hiring line. It is free, and any Texas employer can join.

A stronger talent pipeline

From Classroom to Hiring Line.

  1. Tell providers what you needShare the roles, skills and credentials your industry hires for. Providers build Integrated Education and Training around them.
  2. Shape the trainingAdvise through the employer sessions and regional work groups so programs match the job, not a generic curriculum.
  3. Hire from the pipelineMeet graduates with the credential you asked for, through the providers in your region.
